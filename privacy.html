Privacy Policy

Domino Snap · Effective November 17, 2026

Short version: Domino Snap does not collect, store on any server, sell or share any personal data. Your photos are read on your iPhone and never leave it.

Photos and camera

When you take or choose a photo of your tiles, Domino Snap counts the pips with an on-device model. The photo is processed in memory on your iPhone. It is not uploaded, saved by the app, or sent to us or anyone else.

Game data

Player names, scores and game history are stored only on your device, in the app's own storage. Deleting the app deletes this data. We have no access to it.

Purchases

Purchases and subscriptions are handled by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your name, email, or payment details. The app only checks with Apple whether Domino Snap Pro is active on your account.

No tracking, no ads, no analytics

Domino Snap contains no advertising, no third-party analytics or tracking software, and does not link data across apps or websites.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions: see the support page.
