Domino Snap Support

Mexican Train scorekeeper that counts your pips from a photo.

Getting the best count

  • Lay the tiles flat, face up, and shoot from straight above.
  • Use even light and keep shadows (and your hand) off the tiles.
  • Fill most of the frame with the tiles; avoid busy patterned tablecloths if you can.
  • A red number with “?” means the app isn't sure. Tap any number to fix it, and long-press the photo to add a half it missed.

Purchases

The scoreboard is free. Your first game with the camera is free too. Domino Snap Pro (yearly or lifetime) unlocks the camera for every game.

Restore: open the Pro screen and tap Restore purchases. Cancel a subscription: Settings › your name › Subscriptions › Domino Snap. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.
